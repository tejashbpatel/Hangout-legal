Need help, found a bug, or want to report a problem? Email tejashbpatel@gmail.com. We usually reply within 48 hours.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No separate account or password. Hangout uses the iCloud account that is already signed in on your iPhone. If the app asks you to sign in, open Settings, tap your name at the top, and sign in to iCloud.

How do I report something?
Long-press a message and choose Report Message, or open a Hang and tap the ⋯ menu and choose Report this Hang. We review every report.

How do I block someone?
Use the same menus and choose Block. Their messages and Hangs are hidden from you straight away. To undo it, go to your Profile, tap ⋯, then Blocked People.

How do I delete my account?
Go to your Profile, tap ⋯, then Delete Account. This removes your profile, your Hangs, your messages and any hangouts you created.

How long do Hangs last?
24 hours. After that they disappear for everyone.

Someone I invited can't join.
Private hangouts need the exact name and PIN, and the person who created the hangout must approve each request. Invite links expire after 24 hours, and a hangout can be full (the group size limit is set when it is created).
