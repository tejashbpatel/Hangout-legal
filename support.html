Common questions

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Open the app, go to Settings > Delete My Account. This removes your profile and the content you posted. If you can't open the app, email us and we will do it for you.

How do I report something or block someone?

Use the report and block options in the app on a message or a person. Reported content is reviewed and removed within 24 hours if it breaks the rules.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check Settings > Notifications > Hangout on your iPhone, and make sure you are signed in to iCloud.

I can't see my hangouts or friends.

Make sure you are signed in to iCloud (iPhone Settings > your name) and have an internet connection.

How do private hangouts work?

A private hangout is joined by typing its exact name and PIN. The person who created it still has to approve every request.
