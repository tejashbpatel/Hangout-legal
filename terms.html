Terms & Community Rules

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Community Rules

Hangout is for real conversations between real people. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You agree not to post or send:

    • Nudity, sexual content, or anything involving minors in a harmful way
    • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate toward any person or group
    • Violence, self-harm content, or illegal activity
    • Spam, scams, or pretending to be someone else
    • Other people's private information, or photos of people without their consent

Hangouts

The person who creates a hangout decides who joins and can remove people. Private hangouts are joined with their name and PIN, and the creator still approves every request. Do not share what other members post outside the hangout without their permission.

Reporting and Blocking

You can report content or block a person from within the app. Reported content is reviewed, and content that breaks these rules is removed within 24 hours. The people responsible may be removed from Hangout.

Your Content

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you allow Hangout to store it and show it to the people it is meant for (the members of a hangout, your friends, or the friend you send a doodle to). Hangs leave the feed after 24 hours. You can delete your own messages and hangouts at any time.

Your Account and Data

Hangout uses your iCloud account to save your profile and content. Your camera, microphone and photos are only used when you choose to take or attach something. You can delete your account and all of your content at any time from Settings > Delete My Account.
