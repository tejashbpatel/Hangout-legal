Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who we are

Hangout is an iOS app for meeting up and chatting with the people you choose, in hangouts, with photo and short video moments. It is developed by Tejash Patel. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have.

Information we collect and why

    • Profile information you enter: display name, username, emoji or photo, and optionally a bio, tagline, cover photo, interests and a favorite quote. Other people can see the parts of your profile you fill in. Purpose: to run the app and show who you are.
    • Hangouts you create or join: the name, type, whether it is public or private, the private PIN, the members and join requests. Purpose: to let people find, request to join and meet in a hangout.
    • Messages and shared media: chat messages, photos, videos, replies, emoji reactions, plans and your Going / Maybe / Can't answers. These are visible to the members of that hangout. Purpose: so the hangout works.
    • Hangs: photos and short videos you post. Hangs leave the feed after 24 hours. A copy you choose to save to a hangout stays in that hangout.
    • Friends and connections: friend requests and your list of friends, so you can invite people and see when friends are free.
    • Your status: whether you are free, and a short place or note that you type yourself (for example, "at the coffee shop"). We do not track your location.
    • Doodles and photos for the widget: images you send to a close friend so they appear on that person's Home Screen widget.
    • Account identifier: Hangout uses your iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit to store and load your content. We receive an app-specific identifier tied to it. We never see your Apple ID password.
    • Agreement record: when you accept these terms we save the version you accepted, the date and time, and your app-specific iCloud identifier, so we can show that you agreed. It is deleted with your account.
    • Safety information: reports you send and the list of people you block.
    • On your device only: your settings and cached copies of photos and videos so they load faster.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your email address, phone number, home address, precise location, contacts or payment details. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your data. We do not run our own servers: your content is stored in your iCloud account's CloudKit container.

Storage and sharing

Your profile, hangouts, messages and media are stored with Apple in iCloud (CloudKit) so the other people in your hangouts can see them. Apple processes this data under its own privacy policy. We do not share your information with anyone else, except if the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

Permissions

Hangout asks for access to your camera, microphone and photos only when you choose to take or attach a photo or video. Notifications are optional: if you allow them, the app asks iCloud to alert you about new messages, join requests, friend requests and plans. The Home Screen widget shows images that a friend sent you. If we ever collect new kinds of information, we will update this policy before we do.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can edit your profile, leave a hangout and delete your own messages and hangouts at any time. You can delete your account and everything you posted from Settings > Delete My Account. You can also email us and we will remove or send you your data.

Children

Hangout is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and ask you to read and accept it again.
