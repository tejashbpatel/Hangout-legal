Hangout – Privacy Policy

The short version. We don't run our own servers. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites, and the app contains no advertising or analytics tools. What you create in Hangout is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) under our app's container. As the app's developer we can access that container to run the app and handle safety reports.

1. What is stored when you use Hangout

  • Your profile: display name, username, emoji avatar, and optionally a profile photo, cover photo, tagline, interests and a quote.
  • Your content: messages, photos and videos you send in hangouts; Hangs (photos or videos up to 10 seconds, which disappear after 24 hours); reactions; and the list of Moments you choose to save.
  • Hangouts you create: title, description, whether it is public or private, group size, and the PIN for private ones.
  • Connections: friend requests and friends, join requests, who is in each hangout, people you block, and an optional "free right now" status.
  • Safety reports you submit about content or people.
  • An anonymous iCloud-based user ID that links the items above to your account. We do not receive your Apple ID, email address or phone number.

2. Who can see it

  • Other Hangout users can find you in search and see your profile name, avatar, username and profile photos.
  • Members of a hangout can see the messages, photos and videos shared there.
  • Hangs are visible to other Hangout users for 24 hours.
  • Private hangouts are joined by name and PIN and need the creator's approval, but Hangout is not end-to-end encrypted. Please don't share passwords, financial details or other sensitive information in the app.

3. How we use it

Only to run the features you use, to deliver notifications, and to review and act on safety reports. We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or build advertising profiles.

4. Who else is involved

  • Apple: iCloud/CloudKit stores the data and Apple's push service delivers notifications. Apple's privacy policy applies to their services.
  • YouTube: if you use Watch Together, the video plays from YouTube and YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply to that playback.
  • Legal requests: we may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

We do not share your information with any other third parties.

5. Permissions the app may ask for

Camera and microphone (to capture photos and videos), photo library (to pick or save photos and videos), and notifications. All are optional, and you can change them in iOS Settings.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Hangs disappear after 24 hours.
  • Messages and shared media stay until you delete them or the hangout is deleted.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Profile → ⋯ → Delete Account. This removes your profile, your Hangs, your messages, your connections, and any hangouts you created (for everyone in them).
  • Copies other people saved to their own devices or photo libraries cannot be removed by us.
  • Safety reports may be kept for as long as needed to keep the community safe.

You can also email us at the address above to ask about or delete your data.

7. Children

Hangout is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, contact us and we will remove the account.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
